🥶 Freeze Tag 3D

You are one of 20 runners being hunted by 10 red freezers on a huge map. Get touched and you freeze solid! Touch a frozen teammate to thaw them instantly (thawed runners get a 3s speed boost), and they'll do the same for you. Survive until the timer ends, and don't let everyone get frozen at once.

Freezer mode: you are a freezer with 9 AI partners. Chase down all 15 runners and have every one frozen at the same time before the clock runs out, while the runners keep thawing each other.

Coins: runners earn a runner coin every time they unfreeze someone, and freezers earn a freezer coin every time they freeze someone. Spend coins to BUY a kit once and you own it forever. Pick any kit you own at the start of each round and use it for free, as often as you like. Speed is always free. You start with 3 coins. Combos: press Combine kits, tick 2 or more owned kits and buy the combo for 100 coins (+50 coins for each extra kit). Q then fires all of them at once. The 🔑 owner code (same as Sky Ace) gives infinite coins, kits and combos.

Kits: Runners can pick Speed (1🪙), Barrier (2🪙), Super Speed (3🪙), Climber (4🪙, climb building walls for 6s with no dash cooldown), Shield (5🪙, blocks 1 freeze) or Phoenix (1000🪙, frees everyone). Freezers can pick Speed (1🪙), Giant (2🪙, bigger tag radius), Super Speed (3🪙), Teleport (4🪙), Rope (5🪙, drags a runner to you), Snowball (6🪙) or Blizzard (1000🪙, freezes every runner). Enemies never use kits.

2 players online: one device hosts and shares the 4-letter code, the other joins. The guest picks Runner or Freezer with the role buttons, the host picks theirs to start the round, and you both choose your own kit. You can both be runners, both freezers, or one of each. Coins you earn go to your own device.

View: press P to switch between first-person and a view from just behind you.

Maps: Arena, Maze, Castle, City and Forest. Buildings are hollow with rooms (about half have no stairs, so you need Climber to reach those roofs): go in through the door, pass the doorway in the inner wall, take the stairs inside up to the roof and run around on top (freezers follow you up). The Climber kit scales the outside walls instead. Arena, Castle towers, City and Forest cabins all have them. Auto cycles one per level, or pick one with the map button (M).
