🥶 Freeze Tag 3D

You are one of 30 runners being hunted by 10 red freezers on a huge map. Get touched and you freeze solid! Touch a frozen teammate to thaw them instantly, and they'll do the same for you. Survive until the timer ends, and don't let everyone get frozen at once.

Freezer mode: you are a freezer with 9 AI partners. Chase down all 15 runners and have every one frozen at the same time before the clock runs out, while the runners keep thawing each other.

Coins: pick ONE kit at the start of every round and keep it all round. Each use costs coins. Runners earn a runner coin every time they unfreeze someone, and freezers earn a freezer coin every time they freeze someone. You start with 3.

Kits: Runners can pick Speed, Thaw Bomb (explosion that unfreezes), Barrier (a wall freezers can't cross), Ghost, Shield, Teleport, Phoenix (thaws everyone) or Mine, Smoke Screen, Medic, Decoy, Lightning Zap or Fort, Beacon, Tar Pit, Flash, Evade, Armor, Clones, Shockwave, Rally, Aegis or Time Stop. Freezers can pick Speed, Rope (drags a runner to you), Freeze Wave, Magnet, Snowball, Turbo, Stun Blast, Web Trap, Cage, Blink, Goo Trap, Time Warp or Pounce. The AI uses them too.

Maps: Arena, Maze, Castle, City and Forest. Auto cycles one per level, or pick one with the map button (M).

W/S or ↑/↓ run · A/D or ←/→ turn, or click the game and move the mouse (Esc to release) · Space / E dash · Q / F use kit · Enter start
