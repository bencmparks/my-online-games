🏀 Hoops

Sink as many baskets as you can in 60 seconds. Swishes (no rim touch) are worth 3, other baskets 2, and back-to-back baskets build a score multiplier. After 5 baskets the hoop starts moving!

Drag back from the ball like a slingshot and let go, or aim with the mouse and click anywhere away from the ball to shoot toward the pointer. E starts a game.
