🏺 Mancala

Click one of your pits (bottom row) to sow its stones counter-clockwise, skipping the opponent's store. Land in your store for an extra turn; land in an empty pit of yours to capture the opposite pit. The side that runs out of stones ends the game, and the rest go to the other store.
