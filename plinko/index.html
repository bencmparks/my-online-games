🔮 Plinko

Move the mouse (or finger) to nudge where the ball starts, then click or press Space to drop a ball. It bounces down through the pegs into a slot and pays your bet times the slot's multiplier. Higher risk means bigger edge prizes and a worse middle! Earn XP to level up for coin rewards, a payout bonus and bigger bets, and spend coins on upgrades.
