🗝️ The Lost Light is Bright

A pixel-art adventure across 60 areas and 20 realms. The Bright Light has shattered into 20 shards. Open the chest at your door, grab the sword and bring the light back.

Arrows/WASD move · E confirm/continue · I menu (craft, skills, quests) · walk into things to talk, open or fight · monsters chase you slowly · paths north lead to highlands · caves in the wilds lead to dungeons (find keys, beat the guardian) · south gates in villages lead to Brightwick Town (shops, inn, boss arena).
