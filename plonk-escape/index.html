🧑‍🦲 Plonk Escape

Guide bald Plonk to the flag! A/D or arrows to move, W/Up/Space to jump, S/Down to let go of a swinging rope. On a hanging rope, W/S climb and Space jumps off. Touch TNT, then run — it blows up wooden crates (and you, if you stay close)! Buttons open gates; crushers slam down. Push wooden boxes to use as steps up tall walls and to press buttons. Press C (or the Skin button) to change skin: Funcraft, Freeze Tag, Frozen and more. Dodge spikes and saws, and swing across pits on ropes. Touch a flag to set a checkpoint. R restarts the level from the start. 100 levels!
