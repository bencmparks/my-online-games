⚾ Baseball

Step up to bat! Move the mouse (or arrow keys, or drag your finger) to line the bat circle up with the ball, then click (or lift your finger) or press Space to swing just as it reaches the plate. Good timing and aim mean bigger hits. With a runner on, press S (or the Steal Base button) as the pitch is thrown to try to steal — but get caught and it's an out. Each inning you bat, then you pitch and play defence: aim the red crosshair (mouse or arrows), choose Fastball, Curveball or Changeup (1/2/3), and click or press Space when the meter is centered for an accurate pitch. Corners and mixing up pitches fool the CPU; centered, repeated pitches get hit. Then move your fielder onto the red target to catch its hits. A pitch can also hit the batter (free base, for you and the CPU). Turn on Runners: Running (or press R) and your runners take off on every pitch to steal, or leave it on Holding to stay safe. Make your own team (name, colour, logo and flag), play an Exhibition, or enter the 8-team Tournament bracket: win the quarterfinal, semifinal and final! 3 innings per game — outscore the CPU!
