⚾ Baseball

Step up to bat! Move the mouse (or arrow keys) to line the bat circle up with the ball, then click or press Space to swing just as it reaches the plate. Good timing and aim mean bigger hits. With a runner on, press S (or the Steal Base button) as the pitch is thrown to try to steal — but get caught and it's an out. Each inning you bat, then play defence: move your fielder (mouse or arrows) onto the red target to catch the CPU's hits. 3 innings — outscore the CPU!
