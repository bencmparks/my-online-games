🥏 Ultimate Frisbee

Click Start

Nobody guards the player holding the disc: robots mark the other receivers instead. Move with WASD / arrows. You can't run while holding the disc: click where you want to throw it (or press Space to throw at the nearest open teammate). Catch the disc in the right end zone to score. A dropped or intercepted disc is a turnover, and holding it longer than 8 seconds is a turnover too. On touch, drag to move and tap to throw.
