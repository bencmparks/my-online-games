💥 Cannon Blast

Aim the cannon and smash the crate towers to pop every grumpy green monster and hit every bullseye! 💣 TNT blows up and chains, 🌀 portals teleport your ball, and yellow pads bounce things. Pick a cannonball (keys 1–5): Blast, Heavy (smashes, no blast), Bouncy (bounces then explodes), Splitter (splits at the top of its arc) or Mega (huge blast, costs 2 shots). You only get a few shots per level — save shots for more stars (★★★).

Drag on the game to aim (further = more power) and let go to fire · ↑/↓ angle · ←/→ power · Space fire · R restart · Esc menu
