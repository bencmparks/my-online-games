🪓 Ledge Throw

A turn-based puzzle: the whole map is on screen, so plan your route to the 🌀 portal. Every move is one turn. Shoot fires a ledge sideways along the row right below your feet; it sticks to the first wall it hits (shown as a ghost before you shoot). You can only have one ledge at a time. Jump lifts you 2 tiles and holds you in the air for one free action – shoot then, and you land on the new ledge. Avoid pits and spikes; springs launch you up. Aim for par!

Keys: ◀ ▶ / A D move (steps up 1-tile ledges), Shift+◀ ▶ leap 2, W / ↑ / Space jump, S / ↓ wait, Q shoot left, E shoot right, Z undo, R restart, H hint, F fullscreen.
