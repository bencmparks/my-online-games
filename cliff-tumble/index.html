🪓 Ledge Throw

Reach the 🌀 portal at the end of the course! Cross pits, jump spikes, bounce on springs and climb walls. Click, tap or press E to shoot a ledge straight ahead right below your feet (also works in mid-air!) – it sticks to a wall. You can only have one ledge at a time: a new shot removes the old one, even the one you stand on. Shoot at the top of a jump and you land on the new ledge. Move with A/D or ◀ ▶, jump with W / Space, R restarts, F fullscreen.
