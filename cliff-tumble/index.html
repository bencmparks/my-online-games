🧗 Cliff Tumble

Toss Bob down a giant cliff of ledges! ◀ ▶ / A D aims the throw, hold Space (or the button) to charge, release to throw. In the air, steer with ◀ ▶. Icy blue ledges slope away from the wall and drop you down; grassy ledges stop you. Bounce off ledges, grab coins, hit orange bounce pads, and tumble as deep as you can. Spend coins on upgrades.
