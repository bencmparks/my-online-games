🪄 Pigboils Adventchurs

It is your first night at Pigboils, and the candles have gone out while 26 spellbooks have vanished. Create your student, shop for supplies on a walkable Cool Person Airea, get sorted into a House, follow the daily timetable of classes (brew potions, charm your classmates, duel dark creatures) before curfew, visit the village of Pigsmead, make friends with over two hundred students who all have something different to say, and follow the clues across 6 regions and 96 screens to uncover who is behind the mystery of the Hollow King. Then stay for seven years at Pigboils: from Year 2 to graduation in Year 7, each year brings a new mystery, new classes and harder monsters, while students walk between the screens. No monsters in the castle (except in Defence class!), just secrets, but beware the creatures of the Forbidden Forest.

Arrows/WASD move (on a phone use the on-screen pad and buttons; tap the screen or press ✦ on the name row to type your name) · E talk, cast, buy and continue · Space jump (over bushes and pillars) · C clue notebook · T timetable · I inventory (potions: arrows choose, E drinks) · Q or 1-9 switch spell (charms you have cast in Charms class become spells 5-8) · F or 5-8 pick a charm in the Charms Classroom · J mana potion · M world map (pick a visited room and press E to teleport there) · Esc save and quit to the 10-slot menu · R reload last save · N new game (press twice). Classes only run in their timetable slot, and after 21:00 curfew you must be in the Common Room (sleep there after 20:00). The gate on the left of the Great Hall leads to Pigsmead once you have a House. Play Broomball from your House Common Room (after Flying Lessons) as a Chaser, Beater or Seeker, and talk to everyone, since witnesses hold the clues. Progress saves automatically.
