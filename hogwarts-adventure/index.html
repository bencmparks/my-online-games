🪄 Hogwarts Adventure

It is your first night at Hogwarts, and the candles have gone out while 26 spellbooks have vanished. Create your student, get sorted into a House, go to classes, make friends with over two hundred students, and follow the clues across 6 regions and 96 screens to uncover who is behind the mystery of the Hollow King. No monsters in the castle, just secrets, but beware the creatures of the Forbidden Forest.

Arrows/WASD move · Space talk, cast and continue · C clue notebook · Q or 1-4 switch spell · J mana potion · M world map · R reload last save · N new game (press twice). Take classes, play Quidditch from your House Common Room (after Flying Lessons), and talk to everyone, since witnesses hold the clues. Progress saves automatically.
