Free parking next to the Museum, an air conditioned building, and about one hour for a full visit and guided tour.

Holiday season: No Santa Train excursions will be operating in 2024 or for the foreseeable future. Unfortunately we are no longer able to operate these beloved Holiday trains. The Museum and onsite equipment are decorated for the Holidays by Thanksgiving. Come on by!

What you'll see

Museum interior

  • Historic Virginia railroading materials and equipment
  • Authentic architectural fixtures and furnishings
  • Richmond's largest HO scale model railroad
  • Gift shop

Museum grounds

  • Porter tank style steam locomotive
  • Baggage car
  • Caboose
  • Track cars
  • Signals
  • Railroad artifacts

Museum archives

Thousands of historic photographs and documents on the Southern, Norfolk and Western, and other railroads important to Virginia, available by special appointment.
