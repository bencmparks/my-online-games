🐴 Equestrian: The Golden Horseshoe

Chapter 1Fence 0/8Faults 0Time 0.0Falls 0/3

Right/Left arrows (or D/A) gallop faster or slower, but fast galloping drains stamina. Space or tap jumps (hold for higher). Grab carrots in the air to refill stamina. Clear fences, wide oxers, combinations and water jumps; each knocked rail or splash is +4 faults, and 16 faults is a fall. Three falls end the story. Later chapters add rivals, time limits, storms and darkness.
