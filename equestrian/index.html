Ride a show jumping course. Click Start.

Right/Left arrows (or D/A) to gallop faster or slower. Press Space or tap to jump; hold it for a higher jump. Clear every fence and water jump without knocking rails (+4 faults) or landing in the water (+4 faults). Finish with the fewest faults and the best time. Courses get harder. A refused fence is a fall: 3 falls and you are out!
