⚔️ Stick Legion

Build an army of stick figures and destroy the enemy statue! Miners dig gold automatically. Buy units with the buttons or keys 1-4. Q switches your army between Attack and Defend. Scroll with A/D, arrows or by dragging. R calls Arrow Rain in front of your army. 40 levels! New units unlock as you go: Witch (level 4), Berserker (7), Ice Berserker (11) and Necromancer (15), who turns fallen bodies into zombies. Only your surviving miners join you in the next level. Earn 🪙 coins from kills and wins, then buy permanent upgrades below the canvas.
