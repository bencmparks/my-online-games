⚔️ Stick Legion

A birds-eye battle: the whole field is on screen and your army stands in organised ranks (tough fighters in front, archers and witches behind, necromancers at the back, and necromancers march forward with the army). Your miners and necromancers cannot be attacked. Every level opens with its own story chunk. Press M to turn all your necromancers into miners. Miners dig gold automatically. Buy units with the buttons or keys 1-9. Q switches your army between Attack (they march in formation) and Defend. R calls Arrow Rain on the enemy front. 100 levels in 10 story chapters! You are the Blue Brigade, rebelling against the cruel White Warriors. New units unlock as you go: Witch (level 4), Berserker (7), Ice Berserker (11) and Necromancer (15), who turns fallen bodies into zombies, and Creepy Necromancer (19), who turns them into exploding creepers instead. After a win, a fifth of each kind of surviving warrior stays with you for the next level (rounded up), and for every soldier kind you have none of, another fifth of your miners stays; the rest go home. Your soldiers are stick figures with helmets and weapons. Earn 🪙 coins from kills and wins, then spend them in the Armory below the game, where every kind of soldier has its own upgrades (for example the Berserker helmet or the Necromancer arm): Helmet (health), Weapon (damage), Arm (attack speed), Legs (move speed), and for miners Pickaxe (mining speed) and Cart (gold per trip). Every upgrade also makes that soldier look cooler.
