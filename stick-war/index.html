⚔️ Stick Legion

A birds-eye battle: the whole field is on screen and your army stands in organised ranks (tough fighters in front, archers and witches behind, necromancers at the back). Miners dig gold automatically. Buy units with the buttons or keys 1-8. Q switches your army between Attack (they march in formation) and Defend. R calls Arrow Rain on the enemy front. 40 levels! New units unlock as you go: Witch (level 4), Berserker (7), Ice Berserker (11) and Necromancer (15), who turns fallen bodies into zombies. Only your surviving miners join you in the next level. Earn 🪙 coins from kills and wins, then buy permanent upgrades below the canvas.
