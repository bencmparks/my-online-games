🏹 Archery

Shoot 10 arrows at the moving targets. Check the wind before every shot! Bullseye 100, middle ring 50, outer ring 10.

Mouse / drag aim · ↑ / ↓ aim with keys · hold Space / E or hold the mouse button to charge power, release to shoot · on the title screen ↑ / ↓ picks Easy, Medium or Hard. Every point you score is saved as coins: on the title screen press 1–4 or click an upgrade to buy stronger shots, wind control, bigger targets and extra arrows.
