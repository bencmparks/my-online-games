🪖 Tank Duel

Inspired by the Atari classic Combat. Drive around the walls and shoot your rival; bullets bounce! Each tank has 5 lives: a bullet costs 1, a bomb 2 and a fireball 3. Last tank standing wins. Use Edit board to draw your own walls. You can also play on 2 devices: one hosts, the other joins and uses the Player 1 keys.

P1 (blue): A/D turn, W/S drive, Space fire, E bomb, R fireball, Q laser
P2 (red): ←/→ turn, ↑/↓ drive, Enter fire, / bomb, . fireball, , laser (or the robot)

You get 3 fireballs a match. Bombs explode after 2 seconds (3 out at a time). The laser is an instant beam that smashes every wall in its path and does 2 damage to a tank it hits (1.5 second recharge).
