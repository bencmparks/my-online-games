🚀⚽ Turbo Goal Derby 3D

3D 2 vs 2 rocket-car soccer! B switches between car cam and ball cam. W/↑ drive, S/↓ reverse, A D/← → steer, Space/Shift boost. Hit the ball into the orange goal. Grab glowing pads for boost. Derby: ramming an enemy at boost speed demolishes them for 3 seconds — but they can do it to you! First to 5 goals or most goals when time runs out wins (ties go to golden goal). M changes difficulty.
