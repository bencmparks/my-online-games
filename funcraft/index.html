Funcraft

Click the world to look around. Mine ore and coal with left click, craft a furnace at a workbench, then press E beside the furnace to smelt ore into ingots using coal fuel. Away from stations, E eats food, or tames the animal you're looking at (costs 1 meat; wolves, bears and boars only sometimes accept). Tamed pets follow you and can't be collected. Craft a chest from 8 planks, place it, and press E while looking at it to store your items (airplanes, bricks, anything); empty a chest before breaking it. Flowers grow on grassy plains. Craft a door from 4 planks, place it (it is two blocks tall) and press E to open or close it. Craft chairs (2 planks + 1 wood; press E to sit), beds (3 planks + 2 hide; press E at night to sleep until morning) and ladders (3 planks + 1 wood makes 3; hold W to climb, S to climb down). Look for cherry blossom forests, frosty winter forests and orange autumn forests (in newly explored land). Volcanoes with glowing lava craters rise in new land: lava burns you in survival or sends you home, so keep away. Big mine tunnels (9 wide and 8 tall, with wooden supports and torches) run under hills and mountains in new land; look for openings in hillsides. Mines are huge and have ladder shafts up to the surface every 150 blocks and at both ends. New worlds go 40 blocks deeper, with rich diamond, moonstone and gold ore and lava lakes at the very bottom. Your hotbar sits at the bottom of the screen: press 1-9 (0 is the last slot) or scroll the mouse wheel to pick a slot, and press Tab or I to open the full inventory, where you click items to move them between slots. Pick a hat from the Hat menu (top hat, cowboy, party, crown, wizard, beanie, pirate or chef); press P to see yourself wearing it, and other players see it too. Press H or use Home to return to your starting hill, press Y to save a teleport spot where you stand and U to return to it any time, or T to teleport to another player in your lobby (press again for the next one). Craft torches (wood + coal) and left-click wood, leaves, grass or coal while holding one to set it on fire (or press L); fire goes out unless it touches one of those, spreads slowly, and water puts it out. Craft a boat from 5 planks (no workbench needed), face water and press B to sail; Space hops out. Rivers, lakes and ponds are now 4 blocks deep. Rivers and lakes now cross the land with a current that carries swimmers and boats (rivers flow steadily, lakes drift gently). You float half out of the water; hold C to sink and swim under it. Craft a bucket (3 iron ingots), left-click water to fill it, and right-click to pour it out; a water bucket is the only way to put out a fire by hand. Biomes are now much larger, and rivers and ponds freeze into ice in winter forests. Apple, pear, plum, kiwi and cherry trees grow fruit you can pick by breaking leaves. Mine grass for seeds (wheat, carrot, corn, lemon, watermelon), right-click grass or dirt to plant them, and harvest them when ripe (about 40 seconds). Press E to eat any food; each food gives a 10-second power-up (cherry speed, apple super jump, pear fast mining, plum no hunger, kiwi fast swimming, lemon/meat double damage, carrot super sprint, corn wall climb (hold W against a wall), watermelon healing). Animals have 10 health: 10 hits by hand, 8 with a wooden sword (2 planks + 1 wood). Fruit trees only give fruit; plain, autumn and winter leaves give no food. Dig deep for diamonds and moonstone. Craft a helicopter (2 moonstone + 4 diamond) and press R to fly it: Space goes up, C goes down, WASD moves, R gets out. Craft a rocket (10 iron ingots + 4 gold ingots + 4 diamond + 4 moonstone + 6 coal) and press K to blast off to the moon, where gravity is low and you can mine moon rock and moon dust; press K again on the moon to fly home. Under water everything turns blue. In survival mode, zombies join the wolves, bears and boars.

World:

Hold left-click to dig: cracks show how far along you are. Hands are slow; craft pickaxes (stone, ore), axes (wood) and shovels (dirt, sand, snow) from planks, stone or iron ingots to dig faster. The best tool you own is used automatically.

Press Q to throw the selected item (walk over it to pick it up; direct hits on animals collect meat). Hold V to zoom in. Hold Shift to run at double speed. Press G in mid-air to glide once you've crafted a glider (3 wood + 2 hide). Craft an airplane (8 planks + 2 iron ingots) and press O to fly it: look to steer, W speeds up, S slows down, O gets you out. Press P to change the camera: first person, behind you, or facing you.

Hotkeys: press a number key 0-9 to select its item. Select an item, then hold Shift and press 0-9 to assign it to that key.

Play with a friend: one hosts and shares the 4-letter code, the other joins.
