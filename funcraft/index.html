Funcraft

Click the world to look around. Mine ore and coal with left click, craft a furnace at a workbench, then press E beside the furnace to smelt ore into ingots using coal fuel. Away from stations, E eats food, or tames the animal you're looking at (costs 1 meat; wolves, bears and boars only sometimes accept). Tamed pets follow you and can't be collected. Press H or use Home to return to your starting hill, press Y to save a teleport spot where you stand and U to return to it any time, or T to teleport to another player in your lobby (press again for the next one). Craft torches (wood + coal) and left-click wood, leaves, grass or coal while holding one to set it on fire (or press L); fire goes out unless it touches one of those, spreads slowly, and water puts it out. Craft a boat from 5 planks (no workbench needed), face water and press B to sail; Space hops out. Rivers and lakes now cross the land with a current that carries swimmers and boats (rivers flow steadily, lakes drift gently). Craft a bucket (3 iron ingots), left-click water to fill it, and right-click to pour it out; a water bucket is the only way to put out a fire by hand.

Hunger: 100/100

Press Q to throw the selected item (walk over it to pick it up; direct hits on animals collect meat). Hold V to zoom in. Hold Shift to run at double speed. Press G in mid-air to glide once you've crafted a glider (3 wood + 2 hide). Craft an airplane (10 gold ingots + 10 iron ingots + 30 coal for fuel) and press O to fly it: look to steer, W speeds up, S slows down, O gets you out. Press P to change the camera: first person, behind you, or facing you.

Hotkeys: press a number key 0-9 to select its item. Select an item, then hold Shift and press 0-9 to assign it to that key.

Play with a friend: one hosts and shares the 4-letter code, the other joins.

Click the world to begin exploring.
