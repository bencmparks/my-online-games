Funcraft

Click the world to look around. Mine ore and coal with left click, craft a furnace at a workbench, then press E beside the furnace to smelt ore into ingots using coal fuel. Away from stations, E eats food, or tames the animal you're looking at (costs 1 meat; wolves, bears and boars only sometimes accept). Tamed pets follow you and can't be collected. Craft a chest from 8 planks, place it, and press E while looking at it to store your items (airplanes, bricks, anything); empty a chest before breaking it. Flowers grow on grassy plains. Craft a door from 4 planks, place it (it is two blocks tall) and press E to open or close it. Craft chairs (2 planks + 1 wood; press E to sit), beds (3 planks + 2 hide; press E at night to sleep until morning) and ladders (3 planks + 1 wood makes 3; hold W to climb, S to climb down). Look for cherry blossom forests, frosty winter forests and orange autumn forests (in newly explored land). Volcanoes with glowing lava craters rise in new land: lava burns you in survival or sends you home, so keep away. Big mine tunnels (17 wide and 16 tall, with wooden supports and torches) run under hills and mountains in new land; look for openings in hillsides. Mines are huge and have ladder shafts up to the surface every 150 blocks and at both ends. New worlds go 40 blocks deeper, with rich diamond, moonstone and gold ore and lava lakes at the very bottom. Your hotbar sits at the bottom of the screen: press 1-9 (0 is the last slot) or scroll the mouse wheel to pick a slot, and press Tab or I to open the full inventory, where you click items to move them between slots. Pick a hat from the Hat menu (top hat, cowboy, party, crown, wizard, beanie, pirate or chef); press P to see yourself wearing it, and other players see it too. Press H or use Home to return to your starting hill, press Y to save a teleport spot where you stand and U to return to it any time, or T to teleport to another player in your lobby (press again for the next one). Craft torches (wood + coal) and left-click wood, leaves, grass or coal while holding one to set it on fire (or press L); fire goes out unless it touches one of those, spreads slowly, and water puts it out. Craft a boat from 5 planks (no workbench needed), face water and press B to sail; Space hops out. Rivers, lakes and ponds are now 4 blocks deep. Rivers and lakes now cross the land with a current that carries swimmers and boats (rivers flow steadily, lakes drift gently). You float half out of the water; hold C to sink and swim under it. Craft a bucket (3 iron ingots), left-click water to fill it, and right-click to pour it out; a water bucket is the only way to put out a fire by hand. Biomes are now much larger, and rivers and ponds freeze into ice in winter forests. Apple, pear, plum, kiwi and cherry trees grow fruit you can pick by breaking leaves. Mine grass for seeds (wheat, carrot, corn, lemon, watermelon), right-click grass or dirt to plant them, and harvest them when ripe (about 40 seconds). Press E to eat any food; each food gives a 10-second power-up (cherry speed, apple super jump, pear fast mining, plum no hunger, kiwi fast swimming, lemon/meat double damage, carrot super sprint, corn wall climb (hold W against a wall), watermelon healing). Animals have 10 health: 10 hits by hand, 8 with a wooden sword (2 planks + 1 wood). Fruit trees only give fruit; plain, autumn and winter leaves give no food. Dig deep for diamonds and moonstone. Craft a helicopter (2 moonstone + 4 diamond) and press R to fly it: Space goes up, C goes down, WASD moves, R gets out. Craft a rocket (5 iron ingots + 2 gold ingots + 2 diamond + 2 moonstone + 3 coal) and press K to blast off to the moon, where gravity is low and you can mine moon rock and moon dust, or press M to fly to red Mars (mine Mars rock, dust and diamonds); press K or M again there to fly home. Under water everything turns blue. Press Z or middle-click to open the action menu and point, wave, cheer, shrug, dance, bow, sit or lie down (move or jump to stop; other players see it too). In Skyblock, bridge north from your starting island to the small stone platform and walk into its purple portal to reach the Hub (in OneBlock, a similar platform to the north holds a Shop block you can press E on to buy and sell), a protected lobby plaza with a Quest Master (stand near him and press E to hand in items for coins), a Shop (E: sell ores, buy supplies) and portals to the Mines (a big ore slab with richer ore deeper down, including moonstone; its purple portal returns to the Hub) and back to your Skyblock island; your items come with you. Skyblock and OneBlock (pick them at the gateways) are void worlds: in Skyblock you start on a tiny island with a tree and a chest and bridge to other islands; in OneBlock you break a single block that regrows as a new block in phases (plains, forest, caves, desert and snow, mines, treasure, cherry grove, autumn woods, frozen peaks, moon, Mars and a diamond vault) with animals and loot chests along the way; each mode keeps its own world, and falling into the void sends you home. Bedwars (pick it at the gateways) is a survival void game: you start on a red island with your bed (guarded by planks), an iron and gold generator and a shop block (E to buy planks, stone, brick, iron and diamond swords and a water bucket with the iron, gold and diamonds you collect). Bridge to the rival islands (Blue, Green and Yellow), fight their robots (hit them; they chase you across your bridge and drop iron and gold, and respawn every 15 seconds while their bed lives) and break their guarded beds; destroy all three beds to win 150 coins. You cannot break your own bed, and falling into the void respawns you on your island. Walking through a portal now drops you in the middle of the island you arrive on. Chopped trees regrow after about half a minute. In survival mode, zombies join the wolves, bears and boars. Containment Breach (pick it at the gateways) starts in a safe lobby with the Armory block (E to buy and upgrade guns: pistol, SMG, shotgun, rifle) and two portals: green Easy (fewer, slower zombies, 150 coins) and red Hard (more, faster, harder-hitting zombies, 400 coins). The facility behind them is one winding dark hallway of rooms with no forks, so just keep following it: break the 3 glowing keycard blocks, avoid SCP-173 (it only moves when you are not looking at it; guns can't hurt it), shoot the infected zombies, then kill the giant zombie boss to unlock the exit door (press E or click it). Press H to return to the lobby. Select a gun in your hotbar and hold left click to fire.

World:

Hold left-click to dig: cracks show how far along you are. Hands are slow; craft pickaxes (stone, ore), axes (wood) and shovels (dirt, sand, snow) from planks, stone or iron ingots to dig faster. The best tool you own is used automatically.

Press Q to throw the selected item (walk over it to pick it up; direct hits on animals collect meat). Hold V to zoom in. Hold Shift to run at double speed. Press G in mid-air to glide once you've crafted a glider (3 wood + 2 hide). Craft an airplane (8 planks + 2 iron ingots) and press O to fly it: look to steer, W speeds up, S slows down, O gets you out. Press P to change the camera: first person, behind you, or facing you.

2 players on one keyboard: press the "2 Players" button for a split screen (not available while playing online). Player 1 uses WASD and the mouse as usual. Player 2 uses the arrow keys (Up/Down walk, Left/Right turn), , and . to sidestep, ; and ' to look up and down, / to jump, Enter to dig or fight, Right Shift to place a block, [ and ] to change the held item, and \ to eat or use. You share the backpack and world; Player 2 can't be hurt.

Hotkeys: press a number key 0-9 to select its item. Select an item, then hold Shift and press 0-9 to assign it to that key.

Play with a friend: one hosts and shares the 4-letter code, the other joins.
