Funcraft

Click the world to look around. Mine ore and coal with left click, craft a furnace at a workbench, then press E beside the furnace to smelt ore into ingots using coal fuel. Away from stations, E eats food, or tames the animal you're looking at (costs 1 meat; wolves, bears and boars only sometimes accept). Tamed pets follow you and can't be collected. Press H or use Home to return to your starting hill. Craft torches (wood + coal) and left-click wood, leaves, grass or coal while holding one to set it on fire (or press L); fire goes out unless it touches one of those, spreads slowly, and water puts it out. Craft a boat from 5 planks (no workbench needed), face water and press B to sail; Space hops out. Rivers and lakes now cross the land with a current that carries swimmers and boats (rivers flow steadily, lakes drift gently). Craft a bucket (3 iron ingots), left-click water to fill it, and right-click to pour it out; a water bucket is the only way to put out a fire by hand.

Hunger: 100/100

Click the world to begin exploring.
