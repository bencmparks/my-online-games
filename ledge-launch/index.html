🚀 Ledge Launch

Throw platforms at walls and climb to the 🌀 portal! Click or tap to the left or right of you (or press E) to throw a platform straight out from right below your feet. Jump and throw in mid-air to build a staircase up tall walls. A/D or arrows move, W, Up or Space jump. You only have one ledge: throw another and the first one disappears. Wide pits need a ledge on the far pillar, and towers need a mid-air throw, so plan which wall gets your ledge. Avoid the spikes and pits!
