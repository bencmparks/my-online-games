🐖 Pig Escape

You are a pig on a farm – escape through 10 screens! Your friend Lila follows you and is the only one who can help: she digs under cracked fences, smashes crates and squeezes into tiny gaps to press paw plates. Arrows / WASD move, E use / read / ask Lila for help, Q Lila makes a distraction, R restart.
