🐖 Pig Escape

A funny pixel thinking game: escape the farm through 30 screens in real time – farmers and dogs keep patrolling, and if one touches you you go back to the start of the screen. Free Lila with a lever puzzle (read the signs for clues), tunnel between mud patches, and rescue the piglet. Find a translator for each animal (cow, sheep, chicken, duck, goat, horse), bribe toll-gate guards with the food they ask for, and learn jokes from the animals: one horse opens his gate only for the funniest joke. Arrows / WASD move (hold), E use / talk / tunnel, Space pause, Q choose where Lila distracts, R restart.
