🐖 Pig Escape

Escape the autumn farm through 30 screens! Lila follows you: she smashes crates one at a time, digs up mud mounds one pile at a time and digs buried keys out of the mud, and presses paw plates. Chat with the tip pigs, rescue the little piglet (it squeezes through tiny fences), dodge farmers and dogs. Arrows / WASD move, E use / talk / ask for help, Q Lila distracts, R restart.
