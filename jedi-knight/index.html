⚔️ Scar Wars

100 levels. Duel blaster droids and dark lords in a desert canyon, and fly X-wing missions every 5th level. Level 99: destroy the Death Star. Level 6🦙: face Scar. Level 100: defeat Darth Vader. You can only play levels you've unlocked.

Duel levels: W/S move · A/D strafe · ←/→ or mouse turn (click the game to capture the mouse) · Space or click swings the saber and reflects bolts · Shift or F is Force push.
X-wing levels: mouse or WASD/arrows to fly · Space or click fires lasers · shoot enemy bolts out of the air. On touch, drag to move; X-wing lasers auto-fire.

Score: 0 · Health: 5 · Force: 100% · Best level: 1

Pick a level and press Begin.
