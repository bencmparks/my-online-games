🔮 2048 Marble Run

You are a pile of marbles, and the number above it is your mass. Steer with the mouse (or drag on a touch screen). Touch a pile with the same number to merge into a double, touch a smaller pile to eat it, and touch a bigger pile and you are crushed! The marble color tells the size, and eating enough levels you up to the next number. Reach 2048 to win. Dash with Space, click or the button.
