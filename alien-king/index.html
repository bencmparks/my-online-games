👑 Alien King

You are the Alien King, ruler of a tiny planet. Steer with the mouse (or drag on a touch screen), gobble space dust and asteroids, and swallow smaller rival planets to grow. Bigger planets will swallow you! It is a gentle start: fewer rivals, and they are slower than you. Yellow lightning bolts spread around the map give a free 6-second speed boost. Hold Space, click or press Boost to speed up (it costs a little mass).
