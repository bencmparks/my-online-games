🍔 Cafe Tycoon

Buy a kitchen and a table and customers will show up. Move with WASD / arrows (or tap the room). Stand at the kitchen and press E (or the Action button) to cook the dish a customer ordered, carry it to their table and press E to serve it. They eat, then leave money and trash: press E at the table to collect and clean up. Be quick, hungry customers get angry and leave! Spend your money on more tables, a faster stove, new menu items, ads, and employees (cooks, waiters and cleaners) to run the cafe for you.
