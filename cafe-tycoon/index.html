🐵 Ape Market

Build a giant supermarket! Walk (WASD / arrows, or tap the map) around the big store. Grab fruit, corn, eggs and more from the farms up top and drop them on the matching shelves. Carry raw goods to machines (corn → 🍿, bananas → 🧃, milk → 🧀) and shelve what they make. Catch sneaky 😈 vandals before they spray graffiti on a shelf (clean it by standing there). Rich apes in top hats pay triple and drop 💰 as they walk (grab it!). Ape customers shop their whole list, then line up at a register: stand there to ring them up and walk over the 💵 pile to collect. Stand on glowing pads to unlock new goods, registers, cashiers, helpers, bigger shelves, better prices and more.
