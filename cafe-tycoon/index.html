🐵 Ape Market

Run your own little market! Walk (WASD / arrows, or tap the room) up to the 🍌 banana grove to grab fruit, then carry it to the shelf to stock it. Customers take items, line up at the register and pay: stand at the register to ring them up, then walk over the 💵 cash pile to collect. Stand on a glowing pad to spend your money: unlock corn, eggs and milk, a bigger bag, faster feet, more customers, growth boosts and helpers who restock and run the register for you.
