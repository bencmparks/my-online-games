🙈 Hide and Seek

Seek: find every hider before time runs out. Walk up to a hiding spot and search it. Hide: sneak into a hiding spot and stay out of sight until time is up. The seeker checks spots one by one, and checked spots show a ✓, so slip into one that's already been searched!
