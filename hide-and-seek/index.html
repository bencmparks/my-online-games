🙈 Hide and Seek

A maze of 20 rooms, a new layout every round. Seek: find every hider before time runs out. Walk up to a hiding spot in a room and search it. Hide: sneak into a hiding spot and stay out of sight until time is up. The seeker walks the maze checking spots one by one, and checked spots show a ✓, so slip into one that's already been searched!

3D view: W/S or ↑/↓ walk, A/D or ←/→ turn, or click the game and move the mouse to look around (Esc to release). Top view: WASD / arrows move. V switches view · E / Space search or hide · 1 Seek · 2 Hide
