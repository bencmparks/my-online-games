🪂 Cliff Chucker

Fling poor Bob off the cliff and watch him tumble down the ledges! Set the angle with W/S (or ▲▼), hold Space or the mouse to charge the power, release to throw. In the air, tap Space or click to use a rocket boost. There is no distance limit: the farther he goes, the more coins you earn to upgrade (upgrades never max out).
