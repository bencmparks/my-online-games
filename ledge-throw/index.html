🪂 Cliff Chucker

Fling poor Bob off the cliff and watch him tumble down the ledges! Set the angle with W/S (or ▲▼), hold Space or the mouse to charge the power, release to throw. In the air, tap Space or click to use a rocket boost. There is no distance limit: the farther he goes, the more coins you earn to upgrade (upgrades never max out). You can only see a small circle around Bob, so buy the 🔦 Lantern. Power-ups: 💥 Double Barrel (rockets fire two barrels and you get 2 more rockets) and 🪜 Ledge Maker (press L in the air to drop a springy ledge, 2 at a time).
